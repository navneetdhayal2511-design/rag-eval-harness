# Appeals and Grievances

## Internal appeal

A member may appeal a declined claim or a declined pre-authorisation within
ninety days of the date of the decision letter. The appeal must be submitted in
writing and must identify the decision being challenged and the grounds for the
challenge.

An appeal is reviewed by a clinician who was not involved in the original
decision. Aurora responds to an internal appeal within twenty working days. For
an appeal against a declined pre-authorisation where treatment is time
sensitive, Aurora responds within seventy-two hours.

## Second-level review

Where the internal appeal is unsuccessful, the member may request a second-level
review by the Aurora Medical Review Board within thirty days of the internal
appeal outcome. The Board includes at least one clinician who is not an employee
of Aurora. The Board responds within forty working days and its decision is
final within Aurora's internal process.

## External review

After the internal process is exhausted, or where Aurora has failed to respond
within the time limits above, the member may refer the matter to the independent
Health Insurance Ombudsman. A referral must be made within six months of the
final internal decision. The Ombudsman's determination is binding on Aurora and
is not binding on the member.

Aurora bears the full cost of an external review regardless of its outcome.

## Complaints that are not appeals

A complaint about service quality, waiting times or the conduct of Aurora staff
is handled as a grievance rather than an appeal. Grievances are acknowledged
within two working days and resolved within fifteen working days. A grievance
does not pause any deadline that applies to a claim or an appeal.

## Effect on deadlines

Submitting an internal appeal suspends the one-hundred-and-eighty-day claim
submission deadline in respect of the claim under appeal. The suspension runs
from the date the appeal is received until the date of the appeal outcome
letter.
