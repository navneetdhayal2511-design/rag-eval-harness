# Definitions

The following terms have the meanings set out below wherever they appear in an
Aurora policy document.

## Accident

A sudden, unforeseen and involuntary event caused by external and visible means.
A condition that develops gradually as a result of repeated activity is not an
accident, even where the member can identify the moment the symptoms began.

## Allowed amount

The maximum sum Aurora will treat as an eligible expense for a given procedure
code. The method for determining the allowed amount for an out-of-network
provider is set out in the Provider Network document.

## Day case

A procedure requiring admission to a hospital or clinic for observation or
recovery, where the member is discharged on the same calendar day. An admission
that extends past midnight is an inpatient admission regardless of its planned
duration.

## Eligible expense

A charge for medically necessary treatment that is covered by the member's plan,
is not excluded, is incurred after any applicable waiting period, and does not
exceed the allowed amount.

## Emergency

A sudden deterioration in health that requires immediate treatment to prevent
death, serious impairment of a bodily function, or serious dysfunction of a body
part. A condition that a reasonable person would regard as able to wait for a
scheduled appointment is not an emergency, regardless of how the member
perceived it at the time.

## Medically necessary

Treatment that is consistent with the diagnosis, is the least intensive option
that is clinically appropriate, and is not provided mainly for the convenience
of the member or the provider. A treatment may be medically necessary and still
be excluded.

## Policy year

The twelve months beginning on the policy inception date or on any subsequent
anniversary of it. Benefit limits expressed per policy year reset on each
anniversary and are not pro-rated where a member joins mid-year.

## Pre-existing condition

Any condition for which the member received treatment, took prescribed
medication, or was given a diagnosis in the thirty-six months before the cover
start date. A condition the member was unaware of, and for which no clinician
had recorded a finding, is not a pre-existing condition.

## Treatment

Any medical procedure, consultation, test or prescription undertaken to
diagnose, relieve or cure a condition. Purely preventive measures are treatment
only where the plan schedule expressly provides for them.
