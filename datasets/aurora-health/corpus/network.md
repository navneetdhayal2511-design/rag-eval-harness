# Provider Network and Cashless Treatment

## Network tiers

Aurora operates a two-tier network. Tier One hospitals have agreed tariffs with
Aurora and support direct settlement. Tier Two hospitals have agreed tariffs but
do not support direct settlement, so the member pays and claims reimbursement at
the in-network coinsurance rate.

Any provider that is not in Tier One or Tier Two is out of network and is
reimbursed at the out-of-network rate stated in the member's plan schedule.

## The allowed amount

For an out-of-network provider the allowed amount is the lesser of the invoiced
charge and the median Tier One tariff for the same procedure code in the same
region. Where no Tier One tariff exists for the procedure code, the allowed
amount is the invoiced charge reduced by 25 per cent.

## Cashless treatment

Cashless treatment is available only at a Tier One hospital and only with a
valid pre-authorisation. At admission the member presents the membership card
and the authorisation reference. The hospital collects the deductible and the
member's coinsurance share at discharge and invoices Aurora for the balance.

Where a Tier One hospital refuses cashless treatment despite a valid
authorisation, the member should pay and claim reimbursement, and Aurora will
reimburse the full eligible amount without applying the out-of-network rate.

## Emergency admission to a non-network hospital

An emergency admission to any hospital is reimbursed at the in-network rate for
the first seventy-two hours. After seventy-two hours the member is expected to
transfer to a Tier One hospital where it is clinically safe to do so. Where the
treating clinician certifies in writing that transfer is unsafe, the in-network
rate continues to apply for the remainder of the admission.

The cost of a medically necessary transfer between hospitals is covered in full
under the Emergency Transport benefit and does not count toward the annual
benefit maximum.

## Network changes

Aurora may remove a hospital from the network at any time. Where a member has a
valid pre-authorisation for treatment at a hospital that is subsequently removed
from the network, the authorisation remains honoured at the in-network rate for
ninety days after the removal takes effect.
