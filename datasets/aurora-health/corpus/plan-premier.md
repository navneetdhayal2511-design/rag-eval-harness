# Aurora Premier Plan — Schedule of Benefits

Effective 1 January 2026. The Premier plan is an upgrade of the Essential plan.
Any benefit not expressly varied in this schedule follows the Essential plan.

## Annual limits

The Premier plan carries an annual benefit maximum of 2,000,000 units per
insured person per policy year. The per-person deductible is 2,500 units per
policy year, and the family deductible is satisfied at 5,000 units.

## Coinsurance

After the deductible is met, Aurora pays 90 per cent of eligible in-network
expenses. For out-of-network providers Aurora pays 70 per cent of the allowed
amount. The annual out-of-pocket maximum under the Premier plan is 18,000 units
per person.

## Inpatient care

Room and board are covered at the private room rate. A single accompanying
parent or guardian of an insured child under the age of fourteen is covered for
bed and board for the duration of the admission, to a maximum of 1,200 units per
night.

## Outpatient care

Outpatient consultations are covered at 90 per cent after the deductible with no
annual consultation limit. Physiotherapy is limited to sixty sessions per policy
year under the Premier plan.

## Prescription drugs

Generic medicines are covered in full. Branded medicines are covered at 80 per
cent whether or not a generic equivalent exists. Specialty biologics require
pre-authorisation and are covered at 90 per cent once approved.

## Dental and vision

The Premier plan covers two routine dental examinations and two cleanings per
policy year at 100 per cent. Restorative dental work, including fillings,
crowns and root canal treatment, is covered at 60 per cent up to 30,000 units
per policy year. Orthodontic treatment is covered at 50 per cent for insured
persons under the age of nineteen, to a lifetime maximum of 60,000 units.

Vision cover includes one refraction test per policy year and a spectacle or
contact lens allowance of 4,000 units every twenty-four months.

## Overseas treatment

Planned treatment outside the country of residence is covered at 70 per cent
provided Aurora has issued a written pre-authorisation before the member
travels. Emergency treatment outside the country of residence is covered at
90 per cent for the first ninety days of any single trip, after which cover
ceases for that trip regardless of the member's reason for remaining abroad.
