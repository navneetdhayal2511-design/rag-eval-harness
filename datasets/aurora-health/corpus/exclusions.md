# General Exclusions

These exclusions apply to every Aurora plan. Where a plan schedule appears to
grant a benefit that this document excludes, this document prevails, except
where the plan schedule states that it overrides a named exclusion in this
document.

## Absolute exclusions

Aurora does not cover, under any plan or rider:

1. Cosmetic surgery and any treatment undertaken principally to alter
   appearance, unless it is reconstructive surgery following an accident or a
   covered surgical procedure and is begun within twenty-four months of that
   accident or procedure.
2. Experimental treatment, meaning any treatment that has not received
   regulatory approval for the indication being treated in the member's country
   of residence.
3. Treatment of injuries sustained while taking part in professional sport, or
   while participating in motor racing of any kind.
4. Self-inflicted injury, except where the treating clinician certifies that the
   injury arose from a diagnosed psychiatric condition for which the member was
   under active care at the time.
5. Costs of a companion or visitor, other than the accompanying parent benefit
   expressly provided under the Premier plan.

## Conditional exclusions

The following are excluded unless the stated condition is met:

6. Treatment delivered by a provider who is a member of the insured person's
   immediate family is excluded unless Aurora has given written consent in
   advance of the treatment.
7. Dental treatment arising from an accident is excluded under the Essential
   plan unless the treatment begins within ninety days of the accident, in which
   case it is covered at 80 per cent notwithstanding the Essential plan's
   exclusion of restorative dental work.
8. Weight management treatment is excluded unless the member has a body mass
   index of 35 or above and has completed a documented six-month supervised
   programme, in which case bariatric surgery is covered at the plan's standard
   inpatient rate.

## Exclusions that lapse

9. Treatment of a pre-existing condition is excluded during the waiting period
   set out in the Waiting Periods document and is covered normally thereafter.
10. Treatment arising from an undeclared hazardous occupation is excluded for
    the first twelve months of cover and is covered normally thereafter,
    provided the member has by then declared the occupation in writing.

## Effect of fraud

A claim found to be fraudulent is declined in full. Aurora may in addition
cancel the policy from the date the fraudulent claim was submitted, in which
case benefits already paid in respect of that claim become repayable. Claims
unrelated to the fraud that were settled before that date are not reopened.
