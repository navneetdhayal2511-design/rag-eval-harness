# Waiting Periods

A waiting period runs from the member's cover start date. Treatment received
during a waiting period is not covered, even where the claim is submitted after
the period has ended.

## Standard periods

| Benefit | Waiting period |
| --- | --- |
| Accident and emergency treatment | none |
| General illness | thirty days |
| Pre-existing conditions | twenty-four months |
| Specified surgical procedures | twelve months |
| Maternity | ten months |
| Routine dental and vision | six months |
| Psychiatric treatment | ninety days |

Specified surgical procedures are hernia repair, cataract extraction, joint
replacement, tonsillectomy and the surgical treatment of haemorrhoids.

## Continuity on transfer

A member transferring to Aurora from another insurer without a break in cover of
more than thirty days receives credit for time already served under the previous
policy, up to the full length of the Aurora waiting period. Credit is granted
only for benefits the previous policy also covered. Evidence of prior cover must
be provided within sixty days of the transfer.

## Upgrades between plans

A member upgrading from the Essential plan to the Premier plan serves a fresh
waiting period only for benefits that the Essential plan did not provide. For a
benefit that both plans provide at different levels, the member is covered at
the Essential level until the Premier waiting period for that benefit has been
served, and at the Premier level thereafter.

## Reinstatement after lapse

Where cover lapses for non-payment and is reinstated within forty-five days, no
new waiting period applies and cover is treated as continuous, but treatment
received during the lapsed interval is never covered. Where reinstatement occurs
after forty-five days, every waiting period restarts in full.

## Newborn children

A child born to an insured member is covered from birth with no waiting period,
provided the member adds the child to the policy within thirty days of the
birth. A child added after thirty days is subject to the standard waiting
periods applicable to a new member.
