# Aurora Essential Plan — Schedule of Benefits

Effective 1 January 2026. This schedule must be read together with the General
Exclusions document and the Definitions document. Where this schedule and the
General Exclusions conflict, the General Exclusions prevail.

## Annual limits

The Essential plan carries an annual benefit maximum of 500,000 units per
insured person per policy year. Amounts paid under the Emergency Transport
benefit do not count toward the annual benefit maximum.

The per-person deductible is 7,500 units per policy year. A family covered
under a single policy satisfies its deductible obligation once the combined
eligible expenses of all insured members reach 15,000 units in the policy year,
after which no further individual deductible applies to any member.

## Coinsurance

After the deductible is met, Aurora pays 80 per cent of eligible in-network
expenses and the member pays the remaining 20 per cent. For out-of-network
providers Aurora pays 55 per cent of the allowed amount, and any balance billed
above the allowed amount is the member's responsibility and does not accrue
toward the out-of-pocket maximum.

The annual out-of-pocket maximum under the Essential plan is 40,000 units per
person. Deductible payments and coinsurance both accrue toward this maximum.
Premiums, balance billing and penalties for missed pre-authorisation do not.

## Inpatient care

Room and board are covered at the semi-private rate. Where a member elects a
private room without medical necessity, Aurora reimburses the semi-private rate
and the difference is borne by the member. Intensive care is covered in full
subject to the annual benefit maximum.

## Outpatient care

Outpatient consultations are covered at 80 per cent after the deductible, up to
twelve consultations per person per policy year. Diagnostic imaging ordered by a
network physician is covered without a separate consultation limit.

Physiotherapy is limited to twenty sessions per policy year under the Essential
plan. Sessions delivered during an inpatient admission are not counted against
this limit.

## Prescription drugs

Generic medicines are covered at 90 per cent. Branded medicines with an
available generic equivalent are covered at 60 per cent. Specialty biologics
require pre-authorisation and are covered at 70 per cent once approved.

## Dental and vision

The Essential plan covers one routine dental examination and one cleaning per
policy year at 100 per cent. Restorative dental work is not covered under the
Essential plan. Vision cover is limited to one refraction test per policy year;
spectacles and contact lenses are not covered.
