# Claims Procedure

## Notification

A member must notify Aurora of an inpatient admission within forty-eight hours
of the admission, or as soon as reasonably practicable where the member was
incapacitated. Failure to notify within the required period does not by itself
void the claim, but Aurora may reduce the settlement by 10 per cent of the
eligible amount where late notification has prevented it from managing the cost
of the episode.

## Submission deadline

A completed claim must reach Aurora within one hundred and eighty days of the
date of treatment. A claim received after that deadline is declined unless the
member demonstrates that submission was prevented by circumstances beyond their
control, in which case Aurora will consider a claim received within three
hundred and sixty-five days of the date of treatment.

## Required documents

Every claim must include the completed claim form, the itemised invoice showing
the procedure codes applied, and the discharge summary where the claim relates
to an inpatient admission. Claims for prescription drugs must additionally
include the prescription itself. An invoice that states only a total amount
without itemisation will be returned to the member rather than declined, and the
submission deadline is suspended from the date Aurora returns the invoice until
the date the member resubmits it.

## Settlement timetable

Aurora acknowledges receipt of a claim within five working days. A complete
claim is settled within thirty days of receipt. Where Aurora requests further
information, the thirty-day period restarts on the date the last outstanding
item is received.

Interest accrues on any settlement paid later than sixty days after a complete
claim was received, at a rate of 6 per cent per annum calculated from the
sixty-first day.

## Direct settlement and reimbursement

Where treatment is received at a network hospital under a valid
pre-authorisation, Aurora settles the hospital directly and the member pays only
the deductible and coinsurance at discharge. In all other cases the member pays
the provider and claims reimbursement.

Reimbursement is paid in the currency of the policy. Where the invoice is issued
in another currency, Aurora applies the exchange rate published by the central
bank of the policy currency on the date of treatment, not the date of payment.

## Coordination with other cover

Where an insured person holds other insurance covering the same expense, Aurora
pays its proportionate share based on the ratio of its own annual benefit
maximum to the combined annual maxima of all policies in force. The member must
disclose other cover at the time of claim.
