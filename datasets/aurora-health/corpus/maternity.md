# Maternity and Newborn Cover

## Eligibility

Maternity benefits are available after the ten-month maternity waiting period
described in the Waiting Periods document. The waiting period is measured to the
date of delivery, not to the date of conception or the date of the first
antenatal appointment.

Maternity cover is provided under the Premier plan only. The Essential plan
covers complications of pregnancy as a general illness but does not cover
routine antenatal care or an uncomplicated delivery.

## Antenatal and delivery benefits

Under the Premier plan, routine antenatal care is covered at 90 per cent up to
fifteen appointments per pregnancy. Two routine ultrasound scans per pregnancy
are covered, and further scans are covered where clinically indicated and
documented.

An uncomplicated vaginal delivery is covered up to 180,000 units. An elective
caesarean section requested without clinical indication is covered up to the
same 180,000 units, with any excess borne by the member. A medically indicated
caesarean section is covered up to 320,000 units.

## Complications

Complications of pregnancy and of delivery are covered under the member's
inpatient benefit and are not subject to the delivery limits above. For the
avoidance of doubt, a caesarean section performed in response to an emergent
complication during labour is treated as a complication and not as a delivery.

## Newborn cover

A newborn child is covered from birth for thirty days under the mother's policy
without a separate premium, whether or not the child is subsequently added to
the policy. This automatic cover is limited to 150,000 units.

Where the child is added to the policy within thirty days of birth, cover
continues without interruption and no waiting period applies. Congenital
conditions diagnosed during the automatic thirty-day period are covered, and are
not treated as pre-existing conditions when the child is added to the policy.

## Fertility treatment

Investigation of infertility is covered at 70 per cent under the Premier plan up
to 40,000 units per lifetime. Assisted reproduction, including in vitro
fertilisation and intracytoplasmic sperm injection, is not covered under any
plan.
